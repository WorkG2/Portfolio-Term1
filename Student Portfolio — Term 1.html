SST Soccer Exculs
Add a photo here

Soccer builds more than fitness — it's team-building, communication, and decision-making under pressure. Drills and push-ups build strength; laps and match play build stamina; time in defense or goal builds durability.

Playing a position like winger or back also builds planning: a winger has to decide whether a pass or a shot is safe, and a defender has to judge when to press forward or hold the line.

My goal for this activity is to grow as a defender — sharper decisions, more durability, better positioning — through the season.
